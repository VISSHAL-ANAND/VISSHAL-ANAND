<p align="left">
I’m a first-year engineering student who spends more time in the terminal than the lecture hall. I’m not interested in chasing tutorials; I’m here to figure out how systems actually hold together when you push them to their limits.
<br><br>
My focus is on the "rugged" side of tech—AI, IoT, and distributed systems. I’m fascinated by how we can make software smarter and more resilient in environments where every byte of memory and millisecond of latency counts.
<br><br>
🚀 <b>Edge Intelligence:</b> Moving the brains closer to the data. I build systems that think locally.<br>
🌐 <b>Full-Stack Craft:</b> Bridging deep backend logic with clean, intuitive interfaces that people actually enjoy using.<br>
⚙️ <b>The Low-Level Grit:</b> I’m obsessed with optimization. If I can make a process 10% lighter by rethinking the logic, I’m doing it.<br>
📡 <b>Resilient Networks:</b> Exploring self-healing architectures that can recover before a human even notices a fault.<br>
🧪 <b>The Loop:</b> Build, break, analyze, and iterate. If I didn't break it, I probably didn't learn anything.
<br><br>
I’m a ground-up thinker. I’d rather struggle with a protocol from scratch than copy-paste a solution—because the "under the hood" details are where the real engineering happens.
</p>
